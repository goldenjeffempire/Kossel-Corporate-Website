Build a Premium Corporate Website for Kossel Ltd.

You are an expert senior frontend engineer, UI/UX designer, and product designer. Build a production-quality corporate website for Kossel Ltd., an engineering, oilfield resources, procurement, construction, installation, industrial maintenance, instrumentation, marine logistics, haulage, and inspection company.

The goal is to create a strong, modern corporate MVP that looks credible enough for an established engineering/oil & gas company.

Design Direction

Do NOT make this look like a generic startup website.

The visual identity should communicate:

- Engineering
- Oil & gas
- Industrial capability
- Reliability
- Safety
- Professionalism
- Technical expertise
- Corporate credibility

Use a premium industrial aesthetic:

- Deep navy / charcoal
- White
- Steel gray
- Restrained industrial orange accent
- Strong typography
- Large high-quality imagery
- Clean grids
- Generous spacing
- Subtle animations
- Professional cards
- Strong visual hierarchy

Avoid excessive gradients, excessive rounded cards, cartoon illustrations, flashy animations, or overly futuristic styling.

The design should feel comparable to a high-end engineering/oilfield corporate website.

Reference Material

I have provided images/brochure materials from Kossel Ltd.

Use the provided materials as the primary visual/content reference.

Important:

- Do not invent certifications.
- Do not invent clients.
- Do not invent projects.
- Do not invent addresses, phone numbers, emails, statistics, or company claims.
- If information is missing, create a clearly marked placeholder that can easily be replaced.
- Preserve the actual company identity and services shown in the supplied materials.
- Do not simply reproduce the old brochure design. Modernize it substantially.

Technology

Use:

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Modern component architecture
- Responsive design
- Accessible semantic HTML
- Optimized images
- Clean reusable components

Structure the project so that the website can later be connected to a backend/CMS.

Prioritize:

1. Strong typography
2. Excellent spacing
3. High-quality imagery
4. Clear information hierarchy
5. Premium navigation
6. Professional service presentation
7. Strong CTA placement
8. Mobile responsiveness
